Questions? Answers.

Do I need any design skills or software?+

No. Pick a design, send us your names, dates, venues and photos on WhatsApp, and we set everything. Your guests need nothing either: one tap on the link and it opens.

What exactly do I get?+

A private invitation website at your own link, in English, Hindi and Punjabi, with a tap-to-open reveal, music, your story and photos, every function with maps and calendar buttons, RSVP, shagun by UPI, travel notes for guests abroad, a live stream link, and a personal link and WhatsApp message for each family.

How soon will it be ready?+

Within 3 days of receiving your details. Need it sooner? Tell us; same-day is often possible.

Can I invite different guests to different functions?+

Yes. Each household gets its own link that shows only their functions and greets them by name.

Can I change things after sharing the link?+

Yes, and the link stays the same. Venue changed? Timing moved? We update it and everyone sees the new version instantly.

Does it work on every phone?+

It is built for mid-range Android phones on 4G first, and opens inside WhatsApp and Instagram without installing anything.

Can I add our song and our photos?+

Yes. Your song starts when the invite is opened, with a mute button. Photos go into a gallery your guests can tap to enlarge.

Do you only make wedding invites?+

Weddings are most of what we do, but the same designs work for engagements, receptions, anniversaries, birthdays and baby showers.

Can you make something completely unique?+

Yes. Send us a reference you love and we build an original design in that spirit, with our own artwork.

Do I need to buy a domain?+

No. Your invite lives at our studio link. A custom domain can be added on request.

How do I pay?+

UPI or bank transfer after you confirm the design. We start as soon as the details arrive.

How do I get started?+

Tap any design above and press “Book on WhatsApp”, or just message us.
